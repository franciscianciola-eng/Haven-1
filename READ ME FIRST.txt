HAVEN: HOW TO START IT
======================

First, unzip this folder (on Windows: right-click the zip, then "Extract All"). Then:

  Windows   Double-click "Start Haven.bat".
            If Windows warns you about it, click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  Mac       Double-click "Start Haven.command".
            If your Mac says it can't open it: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
            (On the newest macOS: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, "Open Anyway".)

  Linux     In a terminal, in this folder, run:  ./start-haven.sh

A terminal window opens, and then Haven's window opens in your web browser. Talk to it there.
Keep the terminal window open while you're with Haven. When you're done, press "Rest" in
Haven's window: its life is saved, and it picks up where it left off next time.

THE FIRST TIME takes a while, and needs the internet:
  - a few minutes to install what Haven runs on (1 to 3 GB)
  - then its language area, an open AI model, downloads once. How big depends on your
    computer: 1.5 GB on one with 8 GB of memory, 4 GB on most others, and up to 16 GB
    with a big graphics card. Haven's window shows the progress.
After that it starts in a minute or so.

Replies can take a little while on an ordinary computer (tens of seconds), longer when it
stops to think something over. You can watch its thoughts as it goes.

Everything runs on your computer. Nothing you say is sent anywhere. The internet is only
used for setting up, and for Haven to look things up in the Simple English Wikipedia
when it isn't sure of something.

Where things are kept:
  - Haven's life: the ".haven" folder in your home folder
  - its language area: the Hugging Face cache (".cache/huggingface" in your home folder)
  - everything else: this folder

More about what Haven is and how it works: README.md
